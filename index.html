For Kasakya Ismail, becoming a traditional doctor was not simply a choice of profession—it was a decision to preserve knowledge, values and practices passed down through generations. Growing up with an appreciation for traditional knowledge, he became interested in how communities have historically approached personal, family and spiritual challenges. He chose this path because it allows him to listen closely to people, understand their individual circumstances and provide guidance rooted in traditional practices. While he could have pursued many other professions, Kasakya believes traditional practice gives him an opportunity to preserve an important part of African cultural heritage while serving people who choose to seek traditional guidance. His approach is built around respect, confidentiality, patience, cultural knowledge and responsible traditional practice. Every person is treated as an individual, and every consultation begins with listening and understanding before any traditional guidance is offered. For Kasakya Ismail, traditional practice is therefore more than work—it is a commitment to his heritage and to serving people through the knowledge and traditions he has chosen to carry forward.
